Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Re-Tooth Dental Practice collects, uses, stores and protects personal information when you use our website.

Last updated: 29 October 2026

1. Introduction

Re-Tooth Dental Practice respects the privacy of people who use this website. We aim to handle personal information responsibly and in accordance with applicable data protection requirements, including South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), where applicable.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, the website may collect the following information:

  • Account information: your name and email address when you register.
  • Login information: authentication information processed through the website's authentication service.
  • Appointment information: your name, email address, requested service, appointment date and time, and any additional message you submit.
  • Technical information: information that may be processed by the website's hosting or service providers when the website is accessed.

Please do not submit medical histories, sensitive health information or other unnecessary private information through general message fields.

3. Why We Use Your Information

Personal information may be used to:

  • Create and manage patient accounts.
  • Receive and manage appointment requests.
  • Allow authorised staff to manage bookings.
  • Communicate with users about their requests.
  • Maintain the operation and security of the website.

Information should only be used for appropriate, stated purposes and handled in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

4. How Information Is Stored

The website uses Supabase for database storage and user authentication. Information submitted through registration and appointment features may be stored in the project's database.

Access restrictions are intended to limit patient information to the relevant user and authorised staff. The effectiveness of these controls depends on the actual database policies and website configuration.

5. Sharing of Information

Personal information may be processed by service providers needed to operate the website, including Supabase for authentication and database services. Such providers may process information according to their services and applicable terms.

Access to appointment and patient information within the website should be limited to authorised purposes. Information will not be sold or shared for unrelated purposes without a lawful consent.

6. Cookies and Technical Data

The website's authentication service use browser storage to maintain login sessions. Hosting providers and integrated services may also process technical information.

The technologies actually used depend on the website's configuration. This policy shall be updated if analytics, advertising cookies or additional tracking tools are introduced.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law and its requirements, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Ask questions about how your information is used.
  • Request deletion or object to certain processing where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, subject to applicable requirements.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for another lawful purpose. Requests should be assessed individually.

8. How Long Information Is Kept

Personal information should be kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when website features, data-handling practices or applicable requirements change. The latest version should be published on this page with an updated revision date.

10. Contact Us

If you have a question or request concerning personal information, contact the person responsible for the Re-Tooth website.
