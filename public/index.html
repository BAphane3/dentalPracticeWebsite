Meet Our Team

Dr. Boitumelo Aphane

CEO & General Dentist

Dr. Boitumelo Aphane is a general dentist who focuses on supporting patients with their everyday dental care and oral health needs. His role includes helping patients understand their dental concerns, maintain healthy teeth and gums, and make informed decisions about their treatment options.

Dr. Thando Aphane

Orthodontist

Dr. Thando Aphane qualified with her Bachelor of Dental Surgery in 2018 and shortly afterwards joined the practice under the mentorship of Dr. Boitumelo Aphane. She completed her Postgraduate Diploma in Dentistry (Cum Laude), focusing on Implantology, in 2024. Since then, she has continued to expand her expertise through courses and training in advanced restorative and implant dentistry with the Implant and Aesthetic Academy.

Dr. Omolemo Sedikoe

Cosmetic Dentist

Dr. Omolemo Sedikoe focuses on cosmetic dentistry, which aims to improve the appearance of a patient's smile while considering their dental health and individual needs. Cosmetic dental care may include discussing treatment options that address tooth colour, shape, alignment and overall smile appearance.
